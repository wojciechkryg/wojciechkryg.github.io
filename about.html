about

memolki is a series of free, educational memory card games published on Google Play by Tabula Games Studio, an independent game studio founded by Wojciech Kryg.

how we use Google Ads

We run Google Ads App campaigns to promote our games and acquire new users across Google Search, Google Play, YouTube, and the Display Network.

We use the Google Ads API to programmatically manage video creative assets across our campaigns. With localized promo videos for 32 languages per app variant (128+ videos total), automation is essential for efficient campaign management.
